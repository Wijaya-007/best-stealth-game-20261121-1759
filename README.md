WHITEOUT — a top-down stealth game in an arctic research station. You are a lone
infiltrator in the snow: collect every data core, stay out of the guards' vision
cones, and reach the helipad unseen. Bright snow and ice instead of the usual
night — cover reads at a glance, and a snowstorm gust is your best friend, because
it shrinks every guard's vision.

Three levels, guards with patrol routes and vision cones, a detection meter that
gives you a chance to recover, running noise the guards can hear, crouching,
footprints in the snow, and storm gusts that temporarily blind the patrols. All
art is drawn in code on a canvas and all sounds are synthesised with WebAudio —
no assets, no network requests. Keyboard (WASD/arrows, Shift to run, C to crouch)
and mouse (click to walk to a point).

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`.
